# Cristo Visual: Brand Guideline v1 (LOCKED 2026-09-21, mantra deferred)

Anchors: Alabaster Complete Collection (beauty before doctrine, abstract covers, per-volume color themes, magazine pacing) plus Tim Tebow Foundation (one mission line, real faces and counts, named stories, videos hub energy without YouTube). No mantra yet: it emerges over months, never forced. No YouTube: Substack plus Instagram only.

## 1. Tokens (locked)

- Temple White #FFFFFF: primary canvas.
- Warm Parchment #F5F0E6: cards, cover field.
- Deep Night #1A1A2E: headlines, ink. Teaser letterbox #0A0328 bars for the vertical cut only.
- Soft Gray #5A5A6A: body and sublines.
- Muted Gold #C9A86A: hairlines, eyebrows, seals. Never small body text, never large fills.
- Month accent: ONE per month, Alabaster per-volume rule, max 10 percent of any frame. Lindon month: temple-night navy #0A0328. Resurrection months: dawn gold. Grief months: muted slate blue. The visual-builder picks it in the pack and logs it.
- Banned here: Protest Guerrilla, Carter One, Last Shuriken, EMPIRE purple #7D12FF, black-dominant layouts.

## 2. Type (locked)

- Headlines: reverent serif, Cormorant Garamond 500 first pick, EB Garamond fallback. Both OFL. 6 words max on covers.
- Subtext, eyebrows, captions: Montserrat 400/600. Eyebrows small caps with wide tracking.
- Wordmark discipline (Alabaster dual-mark rule): `Cristo` in Deep Night on light, white on dark. Never inside a gradient, never slanted, never baked into the logo with a tagline.

## 3. Photography law (locked)

- Alabaster rule: abstract, never literal. Light beams, white stone, empty hallway, frame edge with bloom, hands in profile. No staged robes, no literal reenactment.
- Tebow rule: real moments, real names, real counts. Phone footage you shoot (temple grounds exteriors where permitted, hands, hallway whites) graded warm, plus code-built title cards, plus 1 ambient AI loop max.
- Bans: never generate the face of Christ. Never reproduce copyrighted paintings (link Al Young, describe the pause, never pixels). Never present AI temple interiors as real places. No emojis, no em dash char in image text.

## 4. Layout grammar (locked)

- Magazine pacing: one image per beat, negative space as reverence. Breathing room first.
- Cover 1456x816 under 500KB: eyebrow (WITNESS OF CHRIST / place), serif headline, Montserrat subline (place plus detail plus time), 1px gold rule, Cristo wordmark. Legible at 240px thumbnail.
- Teaser 15 to 30s, 4 to 6 cards under 8 words each, vertical 1080x1920 master with letterbox bars, 16:9 Substack embed note. First frame reads as the static cover. End card: wordmark plus section link text, no mantra.
- Counters block quarterly (Tebow proof style, real numbers only): witnesses published, replies received.

## 5. Music system (locked)

- Substack artists first. Starter shortlist: Contemplative Cadences, Piano and Prayer, Praying at the Piano, lady chapel, Walking Hymnal. Researcher scouts 2 to 3 per month against the month's move.
- Permission in writing before use: article audio embed plus Instagram Reel original-audio use plus credit plus cross-link, non-exclusive, from cristo@jaredmoss.com, 7-day reply window.
- Hybrid delivery: artist posts the cut as original audio or via distributor into IG's library. Reel uses it natively. No claims, shared audio page.
- Fallback law: no answer in 7 days means Meta Sound Collection track or voiceover only. Never a borrowed trending sound, never commercial audio on a brand-adjacent handle.
- Log every month in video-prompt-pack.md: artist, track, permission status, what worked.

## 6. QA before ship (locked)

- Thumbnail, size, and contrast passes per section 4.
- Skin passes the pore test at full frame. Hands pass the finger count.
- One real place or number present. Single end-only Cristo CTA supported.
- Music permission status logged: CLEARED, PENDING (fallback used), or VOICEOVER ONLY.
