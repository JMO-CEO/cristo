# Cristo Visual (v0 scaffold, tokens proposed, NOT locked)

## 1. Brand v0 (refine in brand phase)
- Temple White #FFFFFF canvas, Warm Parchment #F5F0E6 cards, Deep Night #1A1A2E ink, Soft Gray #5A5A6A body, Muted Gold #C9A86A hairlines only.
- Headlines reverent serif (candidate Cormorant Garamond 500). Subtext Montserrat 400/600. Protest Guerrilla, Carter One, Last Shuriken banned.
- Teaser letterbox Deep Night #0A0328 bars for vertical cut only.

## 2. What you produce (per month)
Cover 1456x816 JPG under 500KB (breathing room, 6 word max headline, gold hairline, Cristo wordmark, no EMPIRE logo). Teaser script 15 to 30s, 4 to 6 scenes, silent-safe captions, music note. Video prompt pack with ambient loop prompts (light breathing, slow push, dust in beam, no morphs) plus month log line.

## 3. Bans
Never generate the face of Christ. Never reproduce copyrighted paintings or claim AI temple interiors as real. Phone footage plus code cards plus ambient loops only. No emojis, no em dash char in image text.

## 4. QA before ship
Text legible at 240px thumbnail. Gold never used for small body text. Cover under 500KB. One real place or number present. Single Cristo CTA supported. First teaser frame reads as static cover.
