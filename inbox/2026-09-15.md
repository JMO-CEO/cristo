# 2026-09-15 - Lindon Temple Cleaning Pilot

Place: Lindon Utah Temple (under construction / pre-dedication cleaning)
Time: 9:45 PM to 11:45 PM

What happened:
Twenty-five priesthood holders from Midway arrived late evening in white jumpsuits. Stepped inside the unfinished dust and scaffolding. The assignment was cleaning the wood frames and brass hardware before carpet laying, not the glass itself. Standing in the stillness of the unsealed rooms at night. The quiet reverence of working side by side in silence.

WITNESS:
Pausing at the foot of the uncarpeted stairs in the ordinance room. Looking up at the white walls under temporary work lights. A distinct spiritual impression pressed upon my mind: "You are worthy, my son." Not for the vacuum or the cloth in my hand, but simply for being willing to tend the house of the Lord in the dark hours.
