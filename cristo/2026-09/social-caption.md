[WITNESS]

At 9:45 PM, twenty-five men from Midway stepped into the unsealed dust of the Lindon Utah Temple wearing white jumpsuits. Our assignment was simple: clean wood frames and brass hardware before the carpet layers arrived. 

Standing alone near the foot of the uncarpeted stairs in the ordinance room, a quiet impression pressed upon my mind: "You are worthy, my son." 

We often wait until our lives are fully polished before we feel worthy to draw near to the Savior. But He meets us in the dust of our ordinary, tired efforts.
